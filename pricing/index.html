Pricing

Legal AI pricing without per-seat or per-token fees: a machine and a licence

The work stays in the building. So does the bill.

Most legal AI is priced per named seat, with usage metered on top against a credit or token allowance, so a heavy month costs more than a light one. The alternative is to own the capacity: a machine inside the firm and a licence. Enidia is priced that way. The hardware costs about €5,000, the licence is tailored to your firm's volume and matter mix, and there is no metered usage on top.

Two ways to pay for legal AI

Most of the category sells a seat and then meters what you do with it. We sell you the machine. On a task whose whole nature is high volume over long documents, that difference compounds.

What you buy

Fixed cost, unlimited runs

The machine costs what it costs. Running a fifty-question form over three hundred documents costs the same as running it over three, so nobody has to ask permission before using it.

A machine, not a data centre

It runs perfectly well on hardware costing around €5,000. That is one workstation under a desk, which puts it within reach of a three-partner boutique and a single fund administrator, not only of firms with an infrastructure budget.

Scaling costs about €5,000

Double the people using it and you have not doubled a bill, because there is no per-seat bill. If the queue gets long, you add a second machine. That is the whole upgrade path, and you own what you bought.
