For funds, AIFMs and administrators

CSSF self-assessment and sub-fund questionnaires, filled from the fund documents

One task, done properly.

Every year, each Luxembourg fund files its CSSF self-assessment questionnaire, and the auditor checks it. Funds with a December year-end all file between January and April, so the work arrives at once. The answers are in the prospectus and the fund documents; the hard part is finding each one and showing where it came from. The questionnaire for a new sub-fund alone runs to 54 fields in eight sections. Enidia's AssociAte fills the questionnaire from those documents and keeps the page and passage behind every answer.

A CSSF questionnaire for a new sub-fund. 54 fields in eight sections, run over a fund's prospectus. It starts where the work really starts, on the regulator's own download page, and ends with a filled form you could put in front of the CSSF.

How the three statuses work

Taught once

It learns the prospectus's vocabulary

Fund documents rarely use the form's words. Where the tool's own check of an answer against its passage finds a problem, it marks the field uncertain and hands it to you. When it misses one, you tell it once. The lawyer starts with the orange and the grey rows; green rows need only the one-click check.

Uncertain. The document does not use the form's words. The tool answers, shows both passages, and flags the row for your attention instead of hiding its doubt in fluent prose.

Learn. It misses one because your documents use a different word. You tell it, a senior approves the comment, and it finds the same field in the next fund's prospectus. What it learns stays on your server and serves your firm alone.

Questions

CSSF questionnaires, in plain answers

When is the CSSF self-assessment questionnaire filed, and who checks it?

Every year, for each fund. Funds with a December year-end file between January and April, and the auditor checks the answers.

Can it be filled from the prospectus and the fund documents?

Yes. The AssociAte takes the questionnaire and the source documents and answers each question from them. Every answer carries the exact page and the passage behind it.

How do we show the auditor where an answer came from?

The filled questionnaire comes back with, for each element, its status, its verbatim source and its locator. You review and sign it, and the evidence goes with it into the file, to the client, or in front of the auditor.

What happens when the documents do not answer a question?

It comes back marked not found, with the reason: not present in the documents, not yet extracted, or evidence that could not be confirmed. Nothing is left silently blank and nothing plausible is filled in.

Does the questionnaire have to be set up again for each fund?

No. The questionnaire is imported once and becomes a template. Each later sub-fund or year runs the same template over its own prospectus, so the result is the same kind of result every time.

Does it read prospectuses in French or German?

Yes. Documents in English, French or German run together in one pass, and the filled form comes back in one pass.

Do the fund documents leave our premises?

Not in production. The tool runs on a machine inside your network. The free trial runs in our cloud, on a public prospectus or an anonymised document.
