For funds, AIFMs and administrators

CSSF self-assessment and sub-fund questionnaires, filled from the fund documents

One task, done properly.

Every year, each Luxembourg fund files its CSSF self-assessment questionnaire, and the auditor checks it. Funds with a December year-end all file between January and April, so the work arrives at once. The answers are in the prospectus and the fund documents; the hard part is finding each one and showing where it came from. Enidia's AssociAte fills the questionnaire from those documents and keeps the page and passage behind every answer.

A CSSF questionnaire for a new sub-fund. It starts where the work really starts, on the regulator's own download page, and ends with a certificate you could put in front of the CSSF.

How the three answers work

Taught once

It learns the prospectus's vocabulary

Fund documents rarely use the form's words. When it maps one to the other, it says so. When it misses one, you tell it once.

Trace. When the document does not use the form's words, the answer is labelled an inference.

Learn. It misses one because your documents use a different word. You tell it, and it finds the same field in the next fund's prospectus.

Questions

CSSF questionnaires, in plain answers

When is the CSSF self-assessment questionnaire filed, and who checks it?

Every year, for each fund. Funds with a December year-end file between January and April, and the auditor checks the answers.

Can it be filled from the prospectus and the fund documents?

Yes. The AssociAte takes the questionnaire and the source documents and answers each question from them. Every answer carries the exact page and the passage behind it.

How do we show the auditor where an answer came from?

Every run produces a certificate: each element, its state, its verbatim source and its locator. It goes into the file, to the client, or in front of a regulator.

What happens when the documents do not answer a question?

The question comes back marked absent. That is reported as a finding, never left blank and never filled with something plausible.

Do the fund documents leave our premises?

Not in production. The tool runs on a machine inside your network. The free trial runs in our cloud, on a public prospectus or an anonymised document.
