Products

Three specialists, one controlled environment.

AssociAte performs structured legal document review. Librarian makes the firm's own knowledge searchable. Clerk turns counterparty material into structured intelligence. Each is designed to run where the documents already live.

Verified document workflows

AssociAte

Takes a checklist, form, survey, or template and fills it from source documents. Every answer carries its exact passage and page; unanswered questions are reported instead of silently completed.

  • Found, inferred, and absent states
  • Source evidence for every finding
  • Completeness visible across the whole task
Explore AssociAte document-review workflows

Legal knowledge

Librarian

Turns the organisation's own documents into a searchable knowledge base. It retrieves answers, precedents, and their context without sending the collection outside the network.

  • Precedent and clause retrieval
  • Answers grounded in the firm's corpus
  • On-premise deployment
Explore legal knowledge and precedent search

Entity intelligence

Clerk

Builds structured counterparty records from agreements, corporate documents, and registry data, bringing authority, ownership, and obligations into one defensible view.

  • Signing authority and ownership
  • Document and registry evidence
  • Structured records that can be reviewed
Explore counterparty intelligence

How they fit together

One product does the task. Two make it sharper.

AssociAte is the core review product. Librarian contributes the organisation's own precedent and context; Clerk contributes structured information about the entities in the documents.

The common constraint is deliberate: production deployments run on hardware controlled by the customer.
