Legal workflows with a defined answer.

Enidia is built for work where the questions are known, the answers are buried in documents, and somebody must be able to verify both the findings and the gaps.

AssociAte workflows

Verification-first legal document review

These workflows share one engine: a defined request is evaluated against source documents, and every result is returned as supported, inferred, or absent with inspectable evidence.

The common standard
