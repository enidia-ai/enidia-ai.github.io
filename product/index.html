Verification

Three answers, and you can tell them apart

The whole product is that these are three different things, and you can see which is which at a glance.

Found

The document answers the question. The answer carries the page, and the passage it rests on is framed where it sits, so you check it by looking rather than by re-reading.

Inferred

The document says the thing, but not in the form's vocabulary. You get the answer, both supporting passages framed on their pages, and a label saying this is a mapping rather than something the document states.

Absent

We could not find an answer in the documents. That is reported as a finding, never left blank and never filled with something plausible. From there the tool helps you settle the question that actually matters: is it missing from the documents, or did the AI fail to find it?

What comes out: a certificate you can hand over

Every run produces a certificate: each element, its state, its verbatim source and its locator. It goes into the matter file, to the client, or in front of a regulator. The workflow is the same every time: the list arrives, it gets filled, you check it, you argue with it, you correct it, and a certificate comes out.
