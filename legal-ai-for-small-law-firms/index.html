For small law firms

Legal AI for a small law firm: one machine, no minimum seats

Give it the work you'd never give AI.

Most legal AI is sold per named seat with usage metered on top, which makes it expensive for a firm of a few lawyers and turns every large job into a budget question. A small firm does not need that model. Enidia runs on one workstation of about €5,000 under a desk, with a licence and no per-seat bill, so the partner who buys it can use it on every checklist without rationing it.

What a small firm buys

One machine, and nobody has to ration it

A machine, not a data centre

It runs perfectly well on hardware costing around €5,000. That is one workstation under a desk, which puts it within reach of a three-partner boutique and a single fund administrator, not only of firms with an infrastructure budget.

Fixed cost, unlimited runs

The machine costs what it costs. Running a fifty-question form over three hundred documents costs the same as running it over three, so nobody has to ask permission before using it.

Scaling costs about €5,000

Double the people using it and you have not doubled a bill, because there is no per-seat bill. If the queue gets long, you add a second machine. That is the whole upgrade path, and you own what you bought.

Getting started

Watch it. Try it on your own work. Then decide.

Nothing is asked of you until you have seen it work on your documents. There is no trial that expires into a subscription, because the thing you eventually buy is a machine, not a meter.

Questions

For small firms, in plain answers

Is there a minimum number of seats?

No. There is no per-seat bill at all: you buy a machine and a licence, and anyone in the firm can use it.

What hardware does a small firm need?

One workstation, costing around €5,000. That puts it within reach of a three-partner boutique, not only of firms with an infrastructure budget.

What does a heavy month cost?

The same as a light one. Volume changes how long a run takes, not what it costs.

Can we try it before buying anything?

Yes. The trial runs in our cloud on your own checklist and a document you are free to share. Nothing is installed and nothing is signed to find out whether it works.

Do client documents leave the firm?

Not in production. The tool runs on the machine in your office, and nothing is sent to a third-party model.

Try it on one checklist.

Bring a checklist you use every month and a document you can share. It costs nothing to find out.
