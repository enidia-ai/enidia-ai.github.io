Verification-first AI for legal document review, legal knowledge management and compliance workflows.

Give it the work you'd never give AI.

Enidia is verification-first legal AI for document work that law firms, fund teams, and in-house legal and compliance teams cannot afford to get wrong. At its core is checklist automation: give the AssociAte a checklist, survey, regulatory form or template and the source documents, and it performs the legal document review. Every answer carries the exact page and passage behind it; every unanswered item is reported instead of guessed. Source verification and completeness are part of the output, and production deployment stays on hardware you control. The same on-premise platform supports legal knowledge management through the Librarian and structured counterparty intelligence through the Clerk.

Built for three kinds of desk. Law firms: closing and condition-precedent checklists, playbook reviews, due diligence lists. Funds, AIFMs and their administrators: CSSF questionnaires, AIFMD Annex IV, SFDR annexes, prospectus and KIID checks. In-house legal and compliance: DORA and NIS2 supplier clause checks, processor agreements, contract playbooks, CSRD disclosures.

On-premise

The work stays in the building. So does the bill.

On-premise is the part of this that competitors cannot copy without rebuilding their business, and it changes two things at once: who can see your documents, and what a heavy month costs you.

Privilege stays intact

Nothing is sent to a third-party model. There is no processor to add to your record, no sub-processor list to chase, and no transfer to assess, because there is no transfer.

Fixed cost, unlimited runs

The machine costs what it costs. Running a fifty-question form over three hundred documents costs the same as running it over three, so nobody has to ask permission before using it.

A machine, not a data centre

It runs perfectly well on hardware costing around €5,000. That is one workstation under a desk, which puts it within reach of a three-partner boutique and a single fund administrator, not only of firms with an infrastructure budget.
