About Enidia

Who builds Enidia, and where it comes from

Academic rigour. Industry trust.

Enidia was built by three people who worked on legal AI as research before they built it as a product. The research was funded by two grants from the Luxembourg National Research Fund and recognised with a LexisNexis best paper award. The product it became is deployed and independently verified at a Global 10 law firm.
