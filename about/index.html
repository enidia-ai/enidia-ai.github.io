About Enidia

Legal AI built around what a reviewer must be able to verify.

Enidia AI is a Luxembourg company developing on-premise AI for structured legal and financial document work. Its products are designed around inspectable evidence, explicit gaps, and controlled deployment.

The approach

Start with the task, not the chat interface.

Enidia focuses on work with a defined request: a checklist, form, survey, template, knowledge query, or entity record that must be completed from documents.

The product reports what the documents support, what requires an inference, and what could not be found. Production systems run on customer-controlled hardware so confidential material does not need to travel to a third-party model provider.
