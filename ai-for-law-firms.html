Interview · Three questions

What law firms need to hear in the AI wave

Adam Kaczmarczyk, LL.M., Product lead, Enidia AI · Questions by Tomer Libal, CEO
November 4, 2026

Tomer Libal, CEO of Enidia AI, asked our Product lead, Adam Kaczmarczyk, three questions about AI for law firms. Adam holds an LL.M. and also studied data science.

What is the most important message to law firms in the current AI wave?

Firms need to adapt and use the best solutions, with efficiency as the main goal. It must happen within tight requirements of confidentiality and verifiability of the results. Another aspect is independence. AI is becoming the backbone of all processes, and that can make a firm vulnerable if it depends on a token provider. That is why local solutions draw more and more attention.

You studied both data science and law. Why?

I have always been interested in both the humanities and science, and youth is the best moment to develop various skills. The two fields also share logic, analytical skills and factual interpretation. And today, data science can help answer complex legal questions by structuring knowledge and analysing data.

What is the biggest challenge for law firms today?

Briefly: the pace. Processes that used to take weeks can now be solved in days or even hours. To keep up, firms need to integrate AI, with proper planning and analysis, not by picking any tool from the AI wave. They also need to take compliance into account, including data processing and liability for their systems’ results.
