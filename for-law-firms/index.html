Questions

For law firms, in plain answers

Does it work on an LPA or a closing checklist?

Yes. One checklist, one LPA, one pass, and every answer traceable to the clause it came from. Closing and condition-precedent lists come back with each requirement supported, incomplete or absent.

How long does a 25-hour checklist review take with it?

In our own timed run, an LPA checklist that took 25 hours by hand was completed in 4. It is our own figure, not a client's, and no firm has verified it.

Do client documents leave the firm?

Not in production. The tool runs on a machine inside your firm, nothing is sent to a third-party model, and privilege stays intact.

Can we test it on a real matter?

Test it first on a document you are free to share: a public document or an old matter that has been anonymised. The trial runs in our cloud. Confidential client material belongs on the machine in your firm, and only there.

Is it a general legal assistant?

No. It does one task: a fixed list, filled from documents, to a standard somebody can defend. If you want one tool for everything, a general assistant is the better buy.
