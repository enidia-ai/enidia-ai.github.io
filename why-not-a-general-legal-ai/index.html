Why not a general legal AI

Why not a general legal AI for checklists and forms?

General assistants are built for a different shape of work.

The large legal-AI platforms are good products for open-ended work: research a question, summarise a document, draft a first pass, chat with a data room. That is a different job from filling a fixed set of questions and standing behind the result, and the difference is structural, not a matter of quality.

The difference

Four structural reasons

They answer. They do not report coverage.

A general assistant is built to produce an answer to whatever you ask. Ask it fifty questions and you get fifty answers, including for the questions your documents never addressed. The thing you actually need to know on a filing is which answers the documents support, and there is nothing in a chat transcript that tells you.

A missing fact comes back as a confident sentence.

Silence is the expensive failure in this task, and it is invisible in a generative tool: the gap looks exactly like an answer. Our whole design is that the gap is a visible, countable state you have to sign off, not a blank you might never notice.

Citations are not the same as hard evidence.

A page reference next to a paraphrase is not something a reviewer can check without re-reading the document. Every answer here comes with the passage it rests on framed on the page, so checking it takes seconds. That is the only way review at this volume is real review.

Their pricing punishes exactly this workload.

A form of fifty questions against a hundred-page document is the most token-hungry shape of legal work there is. On a per-seat-plus-metered-usage model, the task that most needs automating is the one that costs most to automate, so firms ration it. Ours does not meter, because it runs on a machine you own.

The honest version: if you want one tool for everything, buy one of theirs. If you have checklists, forms or questionnaires to fill and someone has to be able to defend the result, that is the job we built for, and we do it better, cheaper and more safely than a general tool can.
