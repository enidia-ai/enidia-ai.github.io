Solutions

The same verification standard, applied to three kinds of legal desk.

The work changes by market, but the requirement does not: answer a defined set of questions from documents, show the evidence, and make every gap visible.

Law firms

Review that another lawyer can check quickly.

Use AssociAte for due-diligence lists, closing and condition-precedent checklists, and playbook reviews. Findings retain the source passage and page needed for supervision and sign-off.

Explore transaction and closing workflows

Funds and administrators

Structured regulatory work without silent omissions.

Complete CSSF questionnaires and similar regulatory outputs from prospectuses, LPAs, and supporting agreements, while preserving the distinction between supported, inferred, and absent answers.

Explore regulatory questionnaires and filings

In-house legal and compliance

Contract requirements checked consistently.

Review supplier and processor agreements against DORA, NIS2, internal playbooks, or another defined requirement set. The gaps—not a polished summary—remain visible.

Explore contract compliance review

Deployment

Production work stays on hardware you control.

A hosted trial can demonstrate the workflow on material you are free to share. Confidential production documents belong on the on-premise deployment.

That distinction is operational, not cosmetic: it determines where privileged and regulated material can be processed and how costs behave at volume.
